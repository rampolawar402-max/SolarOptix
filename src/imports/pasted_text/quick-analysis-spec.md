Update the existing SolarOptix dashboard by adding a new major section called:

## QUICK ANALYSIS

This should become one of the most important sections of the dashboard.

The purpose is to give the solar owner a fast AI-generated summary of:

- How healthy the solar panels are
- How much money is currently being lost
- Which panels need attention first
- What maintenance should be done
- What problems are likely to occur in the future
- Complete maintenance history through QR codes

Do NOT make this look like a generic AI card.
